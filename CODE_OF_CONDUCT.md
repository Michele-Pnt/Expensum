# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being kind, considerate, and respectful of differing viewpoints.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from experience.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, insults, personal or
political attacks, publishing another person's private information, and other
conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code.
They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that violate it, and may temporarily or permanently exclude a
contributor for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive,
or harmful.

To report a conduct concern, contact a project maintainer privately through
GitHub. Do not post private information in a public issue.
